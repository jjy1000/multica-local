## Why one release instead of two

The working tree already contained the staged-but-uncommitted work for
two distinct releases (0.3.60 user-plugin closure and 0.3.61 squad
subscriber schema fix). Both are already documented in
`.omc/release-notes-0.3.60.md` and `.omc/release-notes-0.3.61.md`, and
both their migrations (166 + 167) are already applied to the live DB.

Shipping two separate .app installs would have meant two cold-start
verifies and two `cp -R` operations for content that ships together
(every plugin run needs the squad-subscriber fix to not 23514).
Consolidating into 0.3.62 keeps the audit chain clean: the
release-notes files retain their original 0.3.60/0.3.61 scope and
contain their own historic context, while the shipped app is 0.3.62.

## Risk vs outcome

- **Risk (low)**: a future grep of `git tag` for `0.3.60` or `0.3.61`
  will not find this ship — those tags never existed (this fork has no
  release tags). The release-notes files under `.omc/` are the
  canonical record.
- **Outcome**: 24 M + 32 ?? working-tree entries collapsed into 3 atomic
  commits, /metrics regression fixed, .app rebuilt and verified.

   → `/Users/jiangjianyan/.multica/backups/pre-update-20260723-XXXX`
   → KB vault rsynced to `~/Documents/.backup/llm-wiki-pre-update-…`
2. Diagnostic: `go test ./...` initially reported three failures.
   - `TestQuickCreateIssueParentTrustBoundary` and
     `TestQoderBackendDoesNotWaitForeverForReaderAfterPromptDone` —
     single-package runs PASS. Cross-package PG pollution, unrelated to
     the staged changes.

   create-dmg 1.2.3 on this fork (long-standing), so we ship the
   `.app` directory directly.
7. `cp -R dist/mac-arm64/Multica.app /Applications/`.

   - `lsof -nP -iTCP:5432 -sTCP:LISTEN` — present within 6 s.
